# FitPulse Help Center (sample, fictional company)

## Membership plans and pricing
FitPulse has three plans. Basic costs $19 per month and includes access to the app and 20 on-demand workouts. Plus costs $39 per month and adds live classes and a personal progress dashboard. Premium costs $69 per month and adds one 1:1 coaching call each month and a custom meal plan. Annual billing gives 2 months free on any plan.

## Free trial
New members get a 14-day free trial of the Plus plan. A payment method is required to start the trial, and it converts to a paid Plus plan automatically after 14 days unless cancelled. You can cancel any time during the trial from Settings > Membership with no charge.

## Cancellation and pausing
You can cancel from Settings > Membership > Cancel plan. Access continues until the end of the current billing period. You can pause your membership for up to 2 months, once per year. Pausing is not available during the free trial.

## Refund policy
Monthly plans are refundable within 5 days of the first charge only. Annual plans are refundable in full within 30 days of purchase. After that, no refunds are given, but you can keep access until the plan ends. Refunds above $150 need approval from a billing manager and take 3-5 business days to reach your card.

## Changing plans
Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date. To change plans go to Settings > Membership > Change plan.

## Live classes
Live classes run Monday to Saturday at 6:30 AM, 12:00 PM and 7:00 PM Eastern Time. Sunday has recovery and stretching sessions only at 10:00 AM. Classes are 30 or 45 minutes long. Recordings are available for 14 days after each class.

## Coaching calls
Premium members can book one 30-minute 1:1 coaching call per month. Unused calls do not roll over. Book from the Coaching tab, up to 48 hours in advance. Coaches can be rescheduled free of charge up to 12 hours before the call.

## Syncing wearables
FitPulse syncs with Apple Watch, Fitbit and Garmin. Open Settings > Devices and choose your device. If heart rate data is missing, make sure Bluetooth is on and the watch app is updated. Whoop and Oura are not supported yet but are on the roadmap.

## Password and login problems
Use "Forgot password" on the login screen. The reset link is valid for 20 minutes. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that sign-in button instead of a password. Accounts are locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed attempts.

## Billing and invoices
Invoices are emailed after every charge and are also in Settings > Billing. To update your card, go to Settings > Billing > Payment method. Failed payments are retried 3 times over 7 days before the account is downgraded to a free account.

## Injuries and medical advice
FitPulse workouts are not medical advice. If a member reports an injury, chest pain or a medical emergency, tell them to stop exercising and contact a medical professional. Support staff must not give medical guidance and these cases are escalated to the safety team.

## Data and privacy
Members can export their workout data from Settings > Privacy > Export. Account deletion requests are handled by the privacy team, not general support, and are completed within 30 days.
